Settings — which post is “higher”?

By default posts are ordered by pay level (Platoon Commander Level-4 → Police Constable posts & Asst. Superintendent Level-2 → Warden, Matron, Escort Level-1). Within the same level, police constable posts come first. Change the order if you think candidates will prefer differently — every rank on this page updates.

How to read “after removal”. CBT-I marks only shortlist candidates; the final merit is decided by CBT-II marks. So the position here is a guide, not a result. If someone above you is also in a higher post's list, there is a good chance they will take that post (if they clear it), so they will not compete with you for your post. Realistic (default) removes only those whose CBT-I position in the higher post is already within its seats — within the open (UR) seats, or within their own category seats. Best case removes everyone who is in any higher list; use it only as the most optimistic view.
