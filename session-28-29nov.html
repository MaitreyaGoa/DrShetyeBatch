Learning

Quick learning notes

Key rules behind the Pre-Test questions. Revise these after the test.

%Profit, mark-up & discount

  • Take CP = 100 to make every calculation easy.
  • Successive changes multiply: SP = CP × (1 + m%) × (1 − d%)
  • Net % for +a% then −b%: a − b − ab/100
Trap: 40% mark-up with 15% discount is not 25% profit. It is 40 − 15 − 6 = 19%.

⚙Time & work

  • Total work = LCM of the given days.
  • Efficiency (units/day) = total work ÷ days.
  • Work done = efficiency × days. Subtract to find the remaining work.

x̄Averages & simple interest

  • Sum = Average × Count. Always convert to sums first.
  • SI = P × R × T / 100. SI is the same every year.
  • Difference of two amounts ÷ difference of years = 1 year's SI.

🚆Trains

  • Crossing a pole or a person: distance = train length.
  • Crossing a platform or a bridge: distance = train + platform.
  • km/h → m/s: multiply by 5/18.

🔢Number series

  • First check the differences. If they double, try × 2 + k.
  • Other common patterns: squares ± n, cubes ± n, alternate series.

🔤Coding–decoding

  • Find the shift for the first 2–3 letters and confirm it is the same.
  • Remember the positions: E5 J10 O15 T20 Y25.

👪Blood relations & directions

  • Read the relation from the end backwards and replace one phrase at a time.
  • Directions: cancel opposite moves (N−S, E−W), then use √(a² + b²).
  • A right turn from North is East, and a right turn from East is South.

⭕Syllogism

  • 'Some A are B' ⇔ 'Some B are A' always follows.
  • 'All A are B' gives only 'Some B are A', never 'All B are A'.
Trap: Two terms linked only through a 'some' statement give no definite conclusion.

✓Subject–verb agreement

  • Neither…nor / Either…or: the verb follows the nearer subject.
  • 'Each', 'every', 'one of the…' take a singular verb.

⇄Active → Passive

  • Keep the tense the same and move the object to the front.
  • will + V1 → will be + V3
  • is/are + V-ing → is/are being + V3
  • has/have + V3 → has/have been + V3

AaVocabulary

  • Meticulous = very careful, precise ↔ careless
  • Scarce = in short supply ↔ abundant
  • Frugal ↔ lavish · Candid ↔ secretive · Obscure ↔ clear
Trap: Antonym options usually include synonyms. Cross those out first.

💬Idioms

  • Burn the midnight oil: work or study late at night
  • Hit the nail on the head: say exactly the right thing
  • Once in a blue moon: very rarely
